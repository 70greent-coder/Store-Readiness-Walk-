How to open the app on your phone
  • 📸 Take Photo works when the file is opened straight from your phone’s Downloads — no website needed.
  • 🎥 Live Scan only works when the app is opened from a secure https:// address. Android blocks the live camera for files opened from Downloads; that is what caused the earlier “Permission Denied”.
  • If Chrome asks “Allow camera?”, tap Allow. If it never asks: Chrome ⋮ → Settings → Site settings → Camera, and make sure it isn’t blocked.
What the barcode can and can’t tell us
  • The long barcode on a beef box (GS1-128) usually carries the product code (GTIN), net weight, pack date, and box serial number.
  • It usually does not carry the manufacturer’s name or the USDA establishment number. The app fills those from what it knows (yellow “check” tag) or leaves them for you to type.
  • For Tyson/IBP boxes, the first 3 digits of the barcode serial number identify the plant, so the app can suggest the EST number. Always compare it with the EST printed on the label.
  • Green tag = read from the barcode. Yellow tag = looked up, please check. Always review before saving.
Security & record-keeping
  • Everything stays on this phone. The app makes no internet connections, uses no cloud services, and sends no photos anywhere. Label photos are used to read the barcode and are not stored.
  • Printed logs are an app-generated reproduction of DeCA Form 40-337 and should be compared with the official form.
