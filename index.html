Operational Standard: Conduct at the start of each operational day. Mark A (Acceptable), AR (Action Required), or IA (Immediate Action). Log all AR and IA exceptions in Section C for ownership, tracking, and verification.

Review pre-walk operational data, shift reports, and carryover items before stepping onto the sales floor.

B. WALK — Minimum Daily Readiness

Score each operational area. Action Req. or Immediate opens an item in Section C.
1. Patron Readiness
Exterior/entrance, carts, cleanliness, restrooms, front-end readiness, registers/SCO.
2. Product Availability & Merchandising
Obvious outs, recovery, promotional displays, endcaps, pricing/signage.
3. Fresh Department Readiness
Produce, meat, deli/bakery: stock, freshness/culling, rotation/dates, presentation.
4. Food Safety / Sanitation / Employee Safety
Temps, sanitation, PPE, slip/trip hazards, exits, immediate safety concerns.
5. Facility & Equipment Readiness
Refrigeration, cases, registers/SCOs, critical equipment conditions.
6. Workforce & Leadership Readiness
Management coverage, staffing/call-outs, workload balance, execution capacity.
7. Corrective Action Follow-Through
Yesterday's open exceptions physically verified; new gaps assigned immediately with an owner and target completion time.

Status Definitions Reference
  • A — Acceptable: Meets established operational standard. No follow-up intervention required.
  • AR — Action Required: Operational deficiency identified. Requires an assigned owner, target due time, and physical verification.
  • IA — Immediate Action: Critical hazard, food safety risk, major customer-flow stoppage, or security condition requiring immediate leader intervention.
